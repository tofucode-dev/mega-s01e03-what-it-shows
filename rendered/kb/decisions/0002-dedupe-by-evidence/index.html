0002 · Proposed replacement: deduplicate by evidence

owner tofucode-dev proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Context

The subject-only rule in decision 0001 hid different mechanisms inside #4471. The rendered DedupeExplorer already contradicts the prose: matching subjects can have different causes, and different subjects can have the same cause. Unknown tenants must not be merged automatically.

Proposed decision

Read bodies, resolve tenant locale, channel and booking-form version, and merge only with evidence of a shared mechanism. Intake tags and browser locale are hints, not authority. Contradictory symptoms stay under investigation. Count unique report IDs, not overlapping export rows. Preserve received and merged timestamps separately. Refresh counts from new exports while the desk keeps working.

Consequences

Triage takes more reading, but unrelated bugs no longer inherit one another’s queued fix. The desk must approve this proposal before it replaces accepted policy. Decision 0001 is unchanged. The incident follow-up records the evidence and separate tickets.
